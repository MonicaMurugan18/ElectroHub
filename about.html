About ElectroHub

Building dependable electronics experiences for India.

Founded in 2018, ElectroHub began as a small customer-first electronics outlet in Chennai and evolved into a trusted omni-channel company serving homes, professionals, and businesses.

Company History

Headquartered in Chennai, India, we have grown through service quality, transparent policies, and long-term customer relationships.

2018: Foundation in Chennai

ElectroHub launched with a focus on curated consumer electronics and honest consultation.

2020: Service Network Expansion

Added in-home installation and authorized repair partnerships across Tamil Nadu.

2023: Enterprise Solutions Division

Introduced bulk procurement and managed device lifecycle programs for businesses.

2026: Nationwide Logistics Coverage

Scaled operations with improved delivery standards and customer support automation.

Mission

To simplify access to reliable electronics by combining premium products, transparent policies, and responsive service.

Vision

To become India’s most trusted electronics solutions company for homes and high-growth businesses.

Core Values

Customer focus, integrity, product authenticity, innovation, accountability, and continuous improvement.
