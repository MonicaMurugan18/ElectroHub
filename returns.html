Returns & Refunds

Simple and transparent policy for eligible orders.

Our return and replacement framework is designed to protect your purchase and ensure a fair resolution process.

Return Policy

  • Eligible products can be returned within 7 calendar days from delivery date.
  • Products must be unused, in original packaging, with all accessories and invoice.
  • Software-activated items, personal care electronics, and sealed consumables may be non-returnable.

Replacement Policy

DOA / Defective Replacement

Devices with verified defects on arrival are eligible for replacement as per brand guidelines.

Transit Damage or Wrong Item

Report within 48 hours with unboxing proof for priority replacement processing.

If Replacement Unavailable

Customers can choose store credit, refund, or an alternate equivalent model.

Refund Process

Step 1: Request Initiation

Submit request with order details from account portal or support desk.

Step 2: Pickup / Verification

Our logistics partner collects product and verifies condition.

Step 3: Approval

After validation, refund is approved within 2 business days.

Step 4: Payment Reversal

Refund is credited in 5 to 7 business days based on payment method.
